AI Concepts Learned
AI Agent
Software that can understand requests, retrieve information and generate responses or actions.

RAG
Retrieval Augmented Generation.

Process:

Question ↓ Retrieve Information ↓ Generate Response

Grounding
Using approved documents as the source of information.

Hallucination
When AI generates incorrect or unsupported information.

Prompt Engineering
Defining instructions that guide AI behavior.

Governance
Controls that ensure AI operates securely and responsibly.
